Why North Bengaluru and Why Now?

The airport corridor

The airport has changed the north of Bengaluru. What was once the city’s edge is becoming a major economic corridor — driven by aviation, infrastructure, employment and limited land supply.

Supply that cannot grow

Ridge-facing land near Nandi is finite and building on it is constrained. Thirty-two villas is the whole project; there is no phase two on this site.

It works as a second home

An hour from north Bengaluru makes this usable on an ordinary weekend, not just a holiday — which is what separates a home you visit from one you keep meaning to.

Tangible, not a promise

Possession is October 2026. The land is here, the villas are taking shape, and what you are buying is tangible — not just a promise on paper.

Yes. The project reaches possession in October 2026, so a viewing walks a finished villa rather than a show unit or a plan. Early morning slots are worth taking between November and February, when the cloud sits in the valley.

The project is approved by leading lenders. Our team can put you in touch with the relevant bank relationship manager.
