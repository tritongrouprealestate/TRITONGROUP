# Creative Strategy and Testing

On modern Meta and increasingly on Google (PMax, Demand Gen, YouTube), creative is the main performance lever. Targeting has become broad and automated. The ad decides who stops.

## Creative strategy framework

For each campaign define:
- **Audience segments by motivation,** not demographics. For example: first home buyer, upgrader, investor, NRI buyer, luxury lifestyle buyer.
- **Angles** per segment: pain, desire, proof, urgency, identity, price or payment plan, location, lifestyle.
- **Formats:** static, carousel, short video (under 30 seconds, hook in first 1 to 3 seconds), walkthrough video, UGC or testimonial, founder or agent to camera, before and after, map or location explainer.
- **Offer:** what makes it worth acting now (limited units, payment plan, site visit incentive, price lock, free consultation).

A concept = one angle + one format + one offer. Test concepts, not tiny variations like button colour.

## Testing method

- Test 3 to 5 new concepts at a time in a dedicated testing campaign or ad set with enough budget. Rough budget per concept: enough to buy 2 to 3x target CPL before judging.
- Judge on cost per qualified lead where possible, backed by leading indicators: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CVR.
- Winners move into the main prospecting campaign. Keep the original post ID when duplicating on Meta to preserve social proof.
- Losers: note why in a creative log so the team learns.
- For clean A/B tests use Meta's A/B test tool or Google Experiments. Otherwise treat results as directional.

## Leading indicators (heuristics, calibrate to your account)

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Hook rate (3s views / impressions) | Is the first frame stopping the scroll |
| Hold rate (ThruPlays / 3s views) | Is the story keeping attention |
| CTR (link) | Is the message and offer compelling |
| CVR on landing page or form | Does the promise match the destination |

Compare against the account's own median, not generic internet benchmarks.

## Fatigue signals

- Frequency rising and CTR falling together over 1 to 2 weeks
- CPM rising while audience has not changed
- Comments turning negative or repetitive

Fix with new concepts, not just new colours on the same ad.

## Copy frameworks

- **PAS:** Problem, Agitate, Solution
- **AIDA:** Attention, Interest, Desire, Action
- **Before, After, Bridge**
- **Proof first:** start with a number, award, testimonial, or result

Copy rules:
- Lead with the benefit to the buyer, not the builder's features
- One clear call to action
- Specific beats generic ("3 BHK, 12 minutes from the airport" beats "premium living")
- Respect platform policy and local advertising regulations (for real estate: registration numbers, disclaimers, no misleading returns claims)
