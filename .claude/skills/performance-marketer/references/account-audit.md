# Account Audit

Score each item: OK, Needs work, Critical. End with the top 5 fixes ranked by money impact divided by effort.

## 1. Measurement (do first)
- Primary conversion is a real business event, not a page view
- No duplicate conversion counting
- Meta CAPI live and deduplicated, Event Match Quality acceptable
- Google enhanced conversions and offline import set up
- CRM captures source and click IDs, lead stage fed back to platforms
- Platform vs CRM lead counts reconcile within a reasonable gap

## 2. Structure
- Not over fragmented (ad sets or campaigns starved of conversions)
- Brand separated from non brand on Google
- Prospecting separated from retargeting, with proper exclusions
- Testing budget exists
- Naming convention consistent

## 3. Targeting and keywords
- Meta: broad or Advantage+ with sensible constraints, existing customers excluded
- Special Ad Category applied where required
- Google: location set to presence, search partners and display reviewed, negatives in place, search terms reviewed recently
- PMax brand exclusions if brand Search runs

## 4. Bidding and budget
- Bid strategy matches data volume
- No campaigns stuck in learning or learning limited for weeks
- Budget concentrated on campaigns with proven cost per qualified lead
- Google lost IS (budget) on profitable campaigns flagged as an opportunity
- Auto applied recommendations off or reviewed

## 5. Creative
- Enough active concepts (not one ad carrying the account)
- Fresh creative launched in the last 2 to 4 weeks
- All key ratios covered (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9)
- RSAs have enough unique headlines, ad strength reviewed but not obsessed over
- No ads with high frequency and falling CTR left running

## 6. Landing pages and forms
- Page loads fast on mobile (check PageSpeed Insights)
- Message match between ad and page
- Form length appropriate, qualifying question present
- Click to call and WhatsApp visible on mobile
- Thank you page fires conversion once

## 7. Lead handling (often the biggest leak)
- Speed to lead measured (target: minutes, not hours)
- Contact rate and qualification rate by source tracked
- Sales feedback loop into marketing weekly

## Output format

1. Executive summary (5 lines)
2. Scorecard table by section
3. Top 5 fixes with estimated impact
4. Quick wins this week vs structural changes this quarter
5. The other side: what in this audit might be wrong because of limited data access
