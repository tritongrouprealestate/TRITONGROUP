# Real Estate and High Ticket Lead Generation

High ticket, long cycle businesses (real estate, education, luxury, B2B) break the usual e-commerce playbook. The purchase happens offline, weeks or months after the click. Optimising for cheap leads is the most common and most expensive mistake.

## The funnel that actually matters

Impression → click → lead → contacted → qualified → site visit or meeting → negotiation → booking → registration or sale

Track conversion rate at every step by source and campaign. Media decisions should use cost per site visit and cost per booking as soon as data allows.

## Quality levers

- **Form friction on purpose.** Higher Intent forms on Meta, a budget or timeline question, OTP or phone verification where available. Fewer but better leads usually win on cost per visit.
- **Optimise for deeper events.** Send qualified and site visit events back to Meta and Google. Move optimisation to them once volume allows.
- **Speed to lead.** Calling within 5 minutes versus hours changes contact rates dramatically. This is often the cheapest improvement available and it is not a media change.
- **WhatsApp and calls.** In many markets buyers prefer chat. Click to WhatsApp ads work if the team replies instantly.
- **Retargeting with proof.** Site visit videos, walkthroughs, construction progress, testimonials, possession updates.

## Creative that tends to work (test, do not assume)

- Walkthrough and drone video with the hook in the first 2 seconds (view, price point, or location claim)
- Location and connectivity explainers (minutes to airport, schools, business district)
- Payment plan and price anchors where regulations allow
- Founder or project head to camera for trust
- Limited inventory and launch phase messaging, only if true
- Lifestyle for luxury, numbers and ROI framing for investors (keep claims compliant)

## Segments to consider

End users (first home, upgrade), investors, NRI or overseas buyers, luxury or second home buyers, and channel partners or brokers. Each needs a different angle and often a different landing page.

## Seasonality and timing

Demand varies by local festivals, financial year end, interest rate changes, bonus seasons and launch phases. Compare against the same period last year, not just last month.

## Compliance reminders (verify locally)

- Special Ad Category (Housing) on Meta in markets where it applies
- Property regulator registration numbers and disclaimers in ads where required (for example RERA numbers in India, or DLD / permit numbers in Dubai)
- No guaranteed return claims
- Accurate pricing, images labelled as artist impressions where applicable

## Benchmarks warning

CPL for real estate varies enormously by city, price band and offer. Never quote a generic benchmark as a target. Use the account's own cost per site visit history, and ask the user for their numbers.

## Useful KPI set for this business

| KPI | Formula |
|---|---|
| Cost per lead | Spend / leads |
| Contact rate | Contacted / leads |
| Qualification rate | Qualified / leads |
| Cost per qualified lead | Spend / qualified |
| Visit rate | Site visits / qualified |
| Cost per site visit | Spend / site visits |
| Visit to booking | Bookings / site visits |
| Cost per booking (CAC) | Spend / bookings |
| Marketing cost as % of sales value | Spend / booking value |
